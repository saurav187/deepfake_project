Paper 34 reports 99.66% accuracy on its OpenVoice corpus. The previously attributed 24.32% belongs to a cited RawNet2 baseline on ASVspoof 2021. Paper 7 expressly retrains on additional corpora. Paper 57 uses ITW internally. Neither an ITW internal accuracy nor a replay t-DCF is interchangeable with external ITW EER. Different accepted trials, frontends, training data, random seeds and operating points preclude ranking small differences as causal.

## Synthesis

* **Representations.** Papers 1, 3, 7, 11, 20, 23 and 48 cover spectrograms, cepstra and engineered features. Papers 5, 12, 13, 15, 18, 37, 41 and 55 reuse pretrained speech representations with substantially different adaptation and fusion costs. Strong transfer occurs with frozen and trainable encoders. Comparing rows cannot isolate the intervention.
* **Efficient models.** Paper 1 demonstrates 1.26M parameters and reports external tests. Paper 54 reports 3.6M parameters and 5 ms per pair. Paper 3’s feature extraction dominates classifier latency. Papers 16, 48 and 59 measure a representation, feature count or quantizer under their own protocols. Model bytes, runtime dependencies and end-to-end time on a named CPU are separate deployment measurements.
* **Language and domain.** Paper 14 directly generates ElevenLabs samples, 18 includes CosyVoice 2.0, 34 uses OpenVoice, 32 tests contemporary codec-based synthesis, and 59 studies RVC. Paper 17 covers Bangla and 44 covers Arabic. These are precedents for modern and non-English detection. Their results leave the bilingual phone operating point unresolved.
* **Validity.** Paper 36 makes nonspeech cues explicit. Papers 9, 32 and 42 manipulate channels or processing, showing why a genuine edited voice needs its own controls. Paper 4 tests localization with time-labelled edits. Papers 1 and 33 consider calibration, 14 reports likelihood-ratio cost, 7 uses resampling-based uncertainty, and 3 includes a speaker-independent protocol. These are precedents to build on.
* **Deployment.** Paper 44 demonstrates a smartphone workflow, 58 hosts a Gradio app and 40 reports inference timing. These answer different questions from running a frozen package entirely on a laptop and validating a low false-alarm rate.
