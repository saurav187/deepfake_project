## Generation and consent

Use development-owned scripts and speaker IDs. Design 160 distinct prompts per language (80 read statements, 40 conversational requests, 20 questions and 20 names/numbers). Group translations, paraphrases and repeats. Assign texts and voices to folds before synthesis. For a starter panel take 40 prompts × 3 voices × 2 languages per service, 240 clips per service. Reserve a fourth voice and distinct text for validation. Ask bilingual speakers to record the same scripts with a comparable delivery. Match true phone transport, device and recording style across labels. Never upload private references to a service without explicit permission for that service.

Microsoft Edge online voices and Azure stock Neural/HD endpoints have different identities. Examples to verify from the owner's accessible voice list are hi-IN-SwaraNeural and en-IN-NeerjaNeural. Keep a multilingual or HD voice held out. Edge's client does not require an Azure key. Free access and sustained availability are unguaranteed. Azure F0 lists 500,000 neural characters/month, subject to account quota and SKU. For 240 clips at 100 characters each the estimated 24,000 characters fit that allowance if unused. [Azure pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/speech/) · [Edge project](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts)

Pin Gemini model and stock voice, e.g. Kore or Puck. At the checked **3.1 Flash TTS Preview** standard rate, 240 × 8 s × 25 tokens/s = 48,000 output tokens, approximately **$0.96 plus input** ($1/M text tokens) on a paid account. The free tier is listed and has quota and data-use conditions. Include unsuccessful/retried generations in accounting. Model choice matters to pricing. [Google pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing)

For local cloning try XTTS-v2 and multilingual Chatterbox on 10 expressly consenting development speakers per language, with 10 novel utterances per speaker per model (up to 400 clips). Reject unavailable language/model combinations. Keep at least two complete speaker families and one model lineage held out. Model licences and recording licences are separate. Public listening rights alone do not grant a person’s permission for a new clone. Store the consent ID, pseudonym, reference lineage, permitted uses, date, withdrawal path and model revision. If consent is absent, use existing released paired fakes or collect a new reference. Budget 0.5–2 GPU-hours as a planning estimate, measure throughput on the first 10. [XTTS-v2 card](https://huggingface.co/coqui/XTTS-v2) · [Chatterbox multilingual card](https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/chatterbox)

Reserve 60–100 previously unseen genuine clips for **test only**: e.g. 20 new participants × 4 messages, balanced across Hindi, Indian English, devices and recording settings. Collect short/long read and spontaneous speech, paired original and WhatsApp-forwarded versions under the same event ID. Obtain separate explicit opt-ins for recording, research scoring, cloning and sharing. Let the custodian keep the speaker key, annotations, matching synthetic controls and deletion requests offline. A sample of 80 real recordings cannot establish a 2% false-alarm guarantee, especially with 20 speakers. Use no product uploads. Keep all of the owner's existing clips sealed.
