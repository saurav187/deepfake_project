# Pipeline review for Deepfake Audio Check

## 1. Verdict

**Modify v2.** Keep the shared 16 kHz decoder, speaker-grouped data, frozen speech encoder and small classifier as the reference system. Spend the next unit of effort on matched genuine and synthetic phone recordings in Hindi and Indian English, followed by an independent calibration split scored exactly as the app will score uploads. The current audit passes on just one training corpus. The legacy results show why lowering false alarms on one simulated channel is insufficient evidence for a public verdict. A frozen encoder is a reasonable academic baseline today. Calling its output a probability for an arbitrary upload, or coloring individual words as synthetic, requires evidence at those respective scales.

## 2. Verified findings, highest impact first

Repository snapshot during the ASVspoof downloads on 23 November 2026. Counts below come from saved artifacts, protocol joins or the stated calculations. Proposed thresholds and compute budgets in later sections are planning assumptions.

1. **The release procedure cannot establish the advertised probability or error budgets.** The best epoch writes its own selection-set predictions. The calibrator fits a sigmoid, chooses both cutoffs and reports performance on those same predictions, counting clean and MP3 versions of each item. Dev samples at most four windows, uses floor division and can omit the tail even below 16 seconds (a 7-second file contributes only its first 4 seconds). The release note says inference will average every window. At review time there was no `detector/inference.py` to verify. There is no fitted segment calibrator. These are three distinct changes of distribution: selection reuse, file length/coverage, and recording-to-window transfer. See `detector/train/train.py:45–71,144–166`, `detector/train/data.py:112–133`, `detector/calibrate.py:160–198`, and PRD §7.1.
2. **A passing audit currently certifies only a narrow property of IndieFake.** The saved `detector/runs/audit/report.json` gives train 1,415 genuine / 1,875 fake across 11 folders and dev 1,603 / 1,921 across **four**. ASVspoof 5 contributes zero rows to that snapshot. Format AUC is 0.5000/0.5001, while grouped duration/silence/level AUC is 0.6991/0.7106. The audit discarded 10,642 IndieFake files for insufficient within-stratum label support alone. It equalizes class fractions within each source and role up to integer rounding, a sound control for those stored fields. Fractions can still differ across sources. The pass criterion checks pooled in-sample stratum AUC, even permits an undefined AUC, and treats the broader probe as a warning. The sampler later draws uniformly from *present* source × label cells. Check completeness, conditional AUC and sampler-weighted pooled AUC independently. `detector/data/audit.py:32–39,115–175,211–226,263–271`; `detector/train/data.py:69–95`.
3. **The launcher can train on one training tar while the second is still pending.** Both the launch and the second download wait on the same two checksum markers. The adapter admits only verified tars and the manifest builder skips previously seen IDs. A later download does not enlarge an already running fit. The run config records a mutable audit path, and calibration hashes whichever manifest exists *at calibration time*. Freeze the inventory, exclusions, sampled IDs, split and hashes alongside each checkpoint at fit time. Also reconcile track-2-only dev enrollment IDs explicitly before loading the final dev tar: the unconditional track-1 lookup can raise a KeyError. See shard evidence below, `detector/run_model_a.sh:15–35`, `detector/datasets/resume_T_ab.sh:3–7`, `detector/data/sources.py:58–81`, `detector/data/build_manifest.py:48–69`, `detector/train/train.py:115–121`, `detector/calibrate.py:199–202`.
4. **The legacy results measure different tasks and populations.** The retrospective fold-2 audit reports only 140 of 2,321 returned rows belonged to the requested fold and that outer-test AUC/labels selected the checkpoint/threshold. Its 0.963 is unusable. The saved historical audit counts 411 duplicate sets. The repaired test has 5,824 files, AUC 0.99390 and EER 4.14%, with ten reserved folder identities. In a separate train-to-dev lookup, weighting each train format's fake fraction by the dev class counts gives AUC 0.9545 from rate and channel count alone. Only 16 kHz mono has both labels in train. Canonical conversion preserves audible correlates of original bandwidth and production history. The historical code at commit `ae01ee0`, `deepfake_model/pipeline/02_train_cnn_lstm.py:392–399,468–478,777–784`, applies a sequential subset sampler to the full dataset and takes the best outer AUC. The weighted lookup recomputes to 0.954488. Evidence includes `deepfake_model/REPORT.md:5–23`, `pipeline/outputs/audited_v1/{audit.json,full/metrics.json}`, and `research/asvspoof_la_v1/source_formats.csv:2–6` under `deepfake_model/`.
5. **Removing one shortcut improved a controlled experiment without solving transfer.** On 734 native-format dev recordings, native versus size-matched mixed training averaged AUC 0.848 versus 0.842, with eight genuine false alarms for each comparison. On the paired call probe, genuine flags went from 0/9 to 7/9 after an 8 kHz round trip and 8/9 after µ-law; only three genuine parents supplied those nine excerpts. Channel augmentation reduced µ-law false alarms from 140.3 to 14.7 per 167 at the respective clean cutoffs. ASVspoof 2021 LA AUC remained roughly 0.61–0.65 across clean/augmented arms. The audited legacy model itself scored 0.622/0.698 for uncompressed/µ-law, with 4,159 unverified trials out of 42,941. Sources: `deepfake_model/research/{training_formats_v1/metrics.csv,call_channel_control_v1/comparison.csv,channel_augmentation_v1/dev_metrics.csv,channel_augmentation_v1/asvspoof_summary.csv,asvspoof_la_v1/summary.csv}` and the accompanying READMEs.
6. **ITW and VA already have a history of inspection.** The archived ITW baseline scored 31,779 files, AUC 0.83928 / EER 23.39%, but detected only 34.86% at its frozen cutoff. Native-clean models ranged from 0.377 to 0.548 AUC. The v2 audit retains 31,712 ITW files after exclusions. Grouping the existing ITW manifest by label gives median stored durations 4.046 s fake and 2.991 s genuine. The saved audit reports 0.785 combined metadata AUC there. Duration affects both which windows are tiled and the opportunity for an extreme score. Historically nine genuine VA files matched IndieFake train; v2 excludes nine IndieFake copies. Exact deduplication cannot resolve edited copies or shared speaker/content identity. Training reads train/dev roles only, but rerunning the audit computes **label-based test probes** on each iteration. Keep structural overlap checks automatic and sequester test diagnostics until the frozen evaluation. No new test predictions were made for this review. `detector/runs/baselines/legacy_itw/summary.csv:2–8`, `detector/runs/manifests/in_the_wild.csv`, audit JSON, `detector/data/audit.py:82–111,211–226`, `deepfake_model/docs/CALL_CHANNEL_PROBE.md`.
7. **Speech duration, timestamps and failure handling need attention before serving.** First-to-last energetic frame includes music and internal gaps. Trimming loses the offset unless it is saved. Short recordings tile periodically, while long tails can overlap the preceding window and receive excess weight. A train read failure returns zero audio with the original label and index −1. The training loop counts it but includes it in the loss. Dev silently drops nonfinite scores and can omit a source lacking both classes. Enforce counts and fail the run on systematic missingness. `detector/audio.py:93–154`, `detector/train/data.py:31–66,121–133`, `detector/train/train.py:58–72,127–151`.

## 3. Recommended pipeline

**Define the prediction target first.** Use speech intervals inside a recording as the unit of evidence. Distinguish widespread synthesis from a localized suspicious interval. File labels suffice for full-utterance training; splice boundaries and genuine edits are needed to validate localization. With the current PRD wording, a single suspicious word in otherwise genuine speech should produce *Can't tell*, with a timestamp and a warning explaining the local evidence. Reserve *Likely AI-generated* for strong, broad evidence compatible with the card's explanation. If the owner wants a probability of *any* manipulation, change the UI definition and recalibrate to that event.

**Data and splits.** Keep official ASVspoof partitions and add matched Indian phone data. IndieFake supplies Indian-accented English; its paper describes English speech. Hindi and code-switching need independently recruited genuine controls. Split source utterance, donor/target speaker, session and all derived edits/codecs as a family before sampling. Use generator-family holdouts, including a genuinely unseen zero-shot system. Record consent, model/version, language, channel chain and manipulation intervals. Sample source × label equally, then balance speakers and generator families within a cell. Skip sources missing a class or supply matched originals. Include genuine re-encoded, edited and denoised audio. [IndieFake paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19014)

**Cheap shortcut checks on development data.** Fit metadata-only classifiers with held-out speakers and report per-source AUC, confusion and counts before/after sampling. Match genuine/fake duration bins and compare fixed-length, unpadded speech. Add/remove equal edge silence, sweep gain and match active RMS/peak. Compare original and equal 3.4/7 kHz low-pass versions; estimate occupied bandwidth from spectra even when both headers say 16 kHz. Pair identical codec chains across labels including MP3→Opus, and check changes for each parent. Predict corpus/language/speaker from embeddings, then measure label performance within corpus, speaker and language and on held-out groups; successful identity decoding alone does not demonstrate label leakage. Fingerprint short overlapping excerpts for cropped/re-encoded duplicates and manually verify candidate pairs. Check donor, transcript and source-video overlap across corpora. Predeclare long/short strata for the final report using dev, never ITW cutoffs.

**Decoder, model and serving.** Decode raw bytes in the detector. Keep original time coordinates and use a lightweight speech activity detector checked against human-marked speech in noisy Hindi and English. Exclude non-speech from the denominator. Use one 4 s encoder window every 2 s as a candidate (299 windows for 600 s), a small bounded GPU batch, and time-weighted overlap accounting. Prefer a mask or valid-length pooling for short inputs. Train on short genuine and synthetic clips as a separate condition. Below a validated speech minimum, including music alone, return *Not enough speech*. With intelligible speech over music, score supported intervals; with competing voices or severe noise, use *Can't tell* when evidence conflicts. No speaker attribution. Warn on unreliable coverage and leave gaps in the timeline.

Start with frozen WavLM Base+ and the existing trainable layer mix, attentive mean/variance pooling and MLP. XLS-R 300M is the **challenger** for Hindi, not a proven winner. Both are already local. Multilingual pretraining is a reason to run a matched comparison, while WavLM is cheaper to iterate and serve. The strongest external justification is for the *family*: Tak et al. found fine-tuned wav2vec plus augmentation effective on ASVspoof 2021, and Combei et al. found both wav2vec and WavLM competitive on ASVspoof 5. Confidence is high about their benchmark findings and low about their ordering on Indian phones. No verified alternative warrants another large encoder download now. [Tak et al.](https://www.isca-archive.org/odyssey_2022/tak22_odyssey.html) · [Combei et al.](https://www.isca-archive.org/asvspoof_2024/combei24_asvspoof.pdf) · [WavLM card](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/wavlm-base-plus) · [XLS-R card](https://huggingface.co/facebook/wav2vec2-xls-r-300m)

On 8 GB, profile peak allocated VRAM, host RSS, decode time and one-minute upload latency first. The current XLS-R layer stack alone at batch 32 is roughly 0.65 GB in float32 (25 × 32 × ~199 × 1024 × 4 bytes), alongside the returned hidden states, model, and temporaries. Try bf16, microbatches 2–4 and gradient accumulation to effective 32. Start with two loader workers and prefetch 1, with a short throughput/RSS pilot against six workers/prefetch 4. Keep random-access tars compressed at the audio level; avoid caching frame embeddings to disk. For supervised adaptation unfreeze the top two blocks or use rank-8 attention adapters, retaining frozen lower layers and checkpointing trainable blocks. Explicitly change the current unconditional no-grad/eval path and save adapted encoder weights. Full XLS-R AdamW tuning leaves little activation margin on this GPU. AASIST or SLS-style layer-specific heads could preserve local artefacts lost in pooling, but require independent gains over the current layer mix. Raw AASIST/RawNet2 from scratch and a small log-Mel CNN are affordable controls with weaker priors on limited labeled data. Add a two-model ensemble only for complementary held-out errors and within the PRD latency budget.

**Channel training.** Retain clean examples and the existing symmetric codec/gain/noise baseline. Test realistic mixtures of single Opus/AAC/MP3 and occasional tandem codecs at recording level, mild room impulse responses, separately permissioned MUSAN-style babble/music, band-limiting and actual device captures. Measure on codec implementations and rooms held out of training. Encode the full recording before the shared prepare step for deployment-like dev views: current DevWindows applies its codec after trimming and normalization, whereas a compressed upload reaches the decoder already encoded. Compare codec delay, clipping and zero-filled edges against clean controls (`augment.py:38–50,64–87`). Aggressive low-pass, impulse noise/RawBoost, strong music, pitch and speed shifts can erase vocoder evidence or generate suspicious artefacts in real speech. Add one family at a time, retain a clean arm, and prefer abstention below intelligibility. Gain can largely disappear after per-window normalization in XLS-R. The in-repo 8 kHz experiment establishes this trade-off better than a published augmentation recipe.

**Aggregation and uncertainty.** Keep the speech-duration-weighted mean logit as the broad-evidence baseline. Also measure the strongest *contiguous* 2–3-window region and its duration. Maximum alone accumulates false alarms across a long clip; a top fraction dilutes a one-word edit; learned attention needs enough labeled mixed recordings to select genuine context controls. Evaluate 2 s and 4 s receptive fields on timestamped splices. Train/calibrate file-level aggregation on the exact serving window schedule, including long genuine calls and sparse edits. Retain original timestamps and score only sufficiently covered regions. Each timeline probability needs calibration against a defined window label (e.g., majority synthetic annotated speech); test event recall separately for short insertions. Until validated, keep localization experimental. Never interpret recording Platt scores applied to windows as calibrated segment probabilities.

**Selection, calibration and confirmation.** Separate speaker/session families into training, selection and calibration. Use grouped cross-fitting if independent cohorts are too small and disclose effective group count. For declared in-scope sources/channels, select by macro fake recall at a common development false-alarm budget, subject to a worst-group genuine false-alarm ceiling and coverage floor. Use macro EER as a secondary ranking measure. Estimate operating points on one group fold and score another. Include actual phone transfers, unseen generator families, partial edits, short clips and genuine edits. Keep all versions of the same content in one fold. Fit Platt once to independent full-recording logits; estimate window calibration separately. The existing equal-cell scheme corresponds to a 50% reference prior when both classes occur per source. For pure prior shift, add logit(target prior) − logit(reference prior) to calibrated log odds. At a 1% prior, a reference 0.9 becomes about 8.3%. This correction requires stable class-conditional scores. For unknown upload prevalence, explicitly identify the reference population. Use *uncertain* with a warning on out-of-domain intelligible speech. PRD §7 allows a null probability only for insufficient speech; suppressing a misleading OOD number requires an owner-approved interface/UI change before public deployment.

Choose separate thresholds for false accusations and false reassurances. Five percent pooled for each is a starting research constraint; report both realized rates, uncertain fractions by class, conditional error among determinate cases and coverage. An all-uncertain detector satisfies both budgets trivially. Use speaker-clustered confidence bounds on an independent validation cohort, retain repeated channel versions together and show unsupported strata. Freeze decoder, weights, manifests, aggregation and calibration by hash before one ITW/VA evaluation. Report per-source, attack/family, language, channel, speech-length and partial/full results, AUC/EER, recall at fixed FPR, actual frozen-threshold FPR/miss, Brier, log loss, reliability plots/ECE with counts, and file plus event coverage. For partials add event recall at fixed false regions per genuine hour, temporal intersection-over-union and boundary tolerance. Bootstrap speakers or parent sessions, with paired resampling for model differences. Small groups merit raw counts and wide intervals.

Reserve an independently collected final phone set *before* experimentation. Recruit new consenting speakers, e.g. 30–50 across Hindi, Indian English and code-switching, multiple phones, sessions, quiet/noisy rooms. Include matched genuine, modern TTS, VC, codec-LM, genuine edits and timed synthetic-word insertions, passed through the same real WhatsApp flows. Reserve complete generator families and capture sessions; keep labels with an independent evaluator. This is a first confirmation, insufficient for precise 1% subgroup claims. A failure there requires a new confirmation cohort after redesign.

**Smallest changes to v2, in order:** pin the actual train inventory and partition dev by family; exclude failed rows from loss/selection and require complete cells; share a single timestamped inference window iterator with calibration; add paired phone dev controls and constrained selection; then implement the PRD adapter with a speech gate, bounded batches, original timestamps, calibrated outputs and experimental flag. Preserve backend size/duration limits, private temporary-file cleanup, restricted FFmpeg inputs and user-safe decode errors.

## 5. Data acquisition order

At 2 MB/s aggregate, **1 decimal GB takes about 8.3 minutes**, before retries or verification. Parallel connections share this budget. HF throughput is unspecified. At inspection, `df -h .` showed 19 GiB free and about 5.7 GiB of RAM was available. Sparse preallocated tar lengths overstate completed downloads. Reserve room for the remaining pieces, the queued tar, checkpoints and OS headroom before scheduling another dataset.

1. **Finish the existing ASVspoof queue.** T_aa 7.52 GB, D_aa 6.65 GB, T_ab 7.49 GB. Total 21.66 GB, about three hours from empty; only remaining pieces count now. Read compressed FLAC members by offset without extracting. For the academic baseline, stop expanding ASVspoof after these. ODC-By covers the database and the bona fide audio is CC BY 4.0; keep attribution and review content rights for the intended service. [Publisher files and local licence counterpart](https://zenodo.org/records/14498691)
2. **Collect the matched Hindi/Indian-English phone pilot first among new sources.** Budget <1 GB locally for the pilot and ≤2 GB for an explicitly licensed generator if needed. Match spontaneous speech, scripts and real delivery channels across classes. Include zero-shot TTS and VC; keep codec-LM examples from a distinct generator for dev. A codec reconstruction alone is a poor proxy for an end-to-end audio LLM. Document voice-owner consent separately for recording, synthesis and release. IndieFake is already local; public-service rights need verification. IndicSynth is a candidate source of Indian-language pairs if speakers, originals and terms can be reconciled. [IndicSynth paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1070.pdf)
3. **Academic generator-diversity pilot.** MLAAD lists 183 GB on HF, CC BY-NC 4.0 and non-commercial academic use. The HF files listing is ~102 GB and the card labels it CC BY 4.0. Check VCTK overlap with ASVspoof 2019 before splitting. Distinguish Lu et al.'s similarly named release with CC BY-NC-ND terms. ## 6. Risks and owner decisions

The hardest failure is a confident “real” on a convincing short splice, or a confident accusation on genuine narrowband speech. A modern generator, new phone DSP or singing voice can sit far outside calibration support. A high SSL score cannot certify provenance. Public deployment needs decisions on the target event, acceptable false accusations versus misses, minimum useful coverage, how to label reference-prior probabilities, and which unsupported inputs may hide the number. Record a rights decision per dataset, pretrained weight and generator before publishing trained weights or exposing a commercial endpoint. Historical VA and ITW results remain context even when sealed from this development cycle. Fresh confirmation speakers are the credible independent final check.
